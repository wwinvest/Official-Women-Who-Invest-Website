What you’ll learn

For middle and high schoolers. No experience needed.

Financial independence
Saving, budgeting, credit, and investing in plain language. What a paycheck turns into, and where it can go.
Intro to business
How companies make money, what the different teams do, and how an idea becomes a business.
Career exploration
What different jobs are really like, and how to work out which ones fit what you’re good at.
Hands-on practice
Build a real budget, pitch a small business idea, and ask the questions you actually have.

How it works

Everything happens online, so you can join from home.

  1. Sign upTell us your grade and what you’re curious about.
  2. Join the sessionsLearn live, or follow along at your own pace.
  3. Try things outBudgets, business pitches, and plenty of questions.
  4. Earn your hoursTime in the program counts toward volunteer hours.

What a session looks like

Short, hands-on, and built around your questions.

Warm-up
A question to get everyone thinking, like what you’d do with your first $100.
Mini lesson
One topic, explained simply. How banks make money, or what a budget is for.
Try it
Put it to work right away with a small activity.
Ask anything
Time at the end for the questions you didn’t want to ask in class.
